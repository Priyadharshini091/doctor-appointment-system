Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions

How do I book an appointment through MediCare Hub?

You can book appointments by searching for doctors in your area using our "Doctor Availability" feature. Select your preferred doctor, choose an available time slot, and complete the booking form. You'll receive a confirmation email with your appointment details.

Is there a fee for using MediCare Hub's services?

No, MediCare Hub is completely free for patients to use. We provide our services at no cost to help connect you with healthcare providers. Any fees would be between you and your healthcare provider for the actual medical services.

How accurate is the hospital wait time information?

Our hospital wait times are updated in real-time directly from our partner hospitals. While we strive for accuracy, wait times can change rapidly in emergency situations. We recommend calling ahead if you're in critical condition.

Can I access my medical records through MediCare Hub?

Yes, if your healthcare provider participates in our medical records program. You'll need to create an account and verify your identity to access your records. Not all providers are connected to this service yet.

How do I report an error in the pharmacy medicine availability?

If you find discrepancies in medicine availability, please use the "Report Error" button on the pharmacy page or contact our support team with the details. We'll verify with the pharmacy and update our records accordingly.
