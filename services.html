Doctor Availability

Our Doctor Availability service helps you find and book appointments with healthcare professionals based on their real-time schedules. No more calling multiple offices to find availability - we aggregate schedules from our partner providers to give you up-to-date options.

  • Search by specialty, location, or specific doctor
  • View real-time availability for the next 30 days
  • Book appointments instantly online
  • Receive email and SMS reminders
  • Reschedule or cancel with one click
Find Doctors Now

Emergency Services

In emergency situations, every minute counts. Our Emergency Services feature helps you quickly locate the nearest appropriate medical facility based on your location and the type of care needed.

  • Real-time hospital wait times
  • Filter by specialty (trauma, pediatric, cardiac, etc.)
  • Turn-by-turn navigation to the facility
  • Emergency contact numbers
  • First aid instructions while you wait
Find Emergency Care

Patient Waiting List

Our Patient Waiting List feature allows you to check your position in the queue before arriving at the clinic, helping you manage your time more effectively and reduce waiting room stress.

  • Real-time queue position updates
  • Estimated wait time calculations
  • Notifications when your turn is approaching
  • Ability to check in remotely
  • Doctor running late alerts

Medical Records

  • View test results as soon as they're available
  • Access vaccination records

  • Track medications and allergies
  • HIPAA-compliant security
